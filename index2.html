Progressive Overload Rules

  • Add weight only when you complete ALL sets at the top of the rep range with clean form
  • Lower body: add 5 lbs · Upper body: add 2.5 lbs
  • Miss reps significantly? Stay at that weight one more week
  • Always start unilateral sets on the LEFT side — match right to whatever left completes
  • Machine weights vary — trust feel, and note which machine you used
